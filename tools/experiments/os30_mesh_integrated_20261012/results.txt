OS30 integrated mesh experiment: measured outcome

NOT DEPLOYED. NO PHYSICAL BUILD OR TIMING QUALIFICATION SELECTED.

The implemented 512-word mesh queue did not make these full native intervals
faster. The final F19 MESH4 adaptive-feed app takes 8,901,507 cycles versus
8,531,290 for its same-ELF capability-off control: +370,217 cycles (+4.340%).
Its warm interval is 4,588,457 versus 4,463,823 cycles (+2.792%).

Each row compares the same app ELF and prepared input with capability off/on.
Rows using different app versions are not interchangeable controls: code layout,
source-cache hashes and the capability-off path also change. The two intervals
include game/render/audio work and complete GPU drains. They use actual native
CPU/raster RTL, fixed synthetic display/audio arrivals and bounded service
implementations; they do not qualify sustained Pocket FPS or the full OS/PCM/
interrupt workload.

The 2048-word diagnostic nearly ties in total (+0.124%), despite a warm gain.
The 16384-word capacity ceiling uses 4.376% fewer cycles in total, but is not a
physically qualified or area-feasible selected core. Its warm peak reservation is 3642
words. The bundle's native-v7 RTL remains the tested 512-word implementation;
these larger diagnostic models are evidence about queue pressure, not bundled
or deployed hardware replacements. Against the earlier qualified app
(8,347,682 cycles), the ceiling's 8,068,806 cycles are 3.341% fewer. That comparison
changes the app too; only the same-ELF rows isolate the architecture. This is a
capacity ceiling for this scene and this architecture, not a universal limit
on accelerator performance.

Adaptive feed did not remove that pressure. Its warm frame has 550,944 cycles
waiting for generated-packet queue credit; 525,251 overlap raster fragment
work. These counters overlap CPU waits and other GPU activity. They are not
additive savings or an FPS estimate. Publishing earlier can feed an empty GPU,
but cannot free packet storage held behind older raster work.

The independent native checks compare frame/depth output, ordered effective
draws and state, Surface data and register/FCSR results. Source-cache omissions
are tied to actual probe/fallback execution; they are not accepted merely
because pixels match. Final receipts identify the exact selected checker
versions and any historical supersession. Source/evidence hashes and external
input bindings are in manifest.json. Earlier receipts retain their original
point-in-time pending fields and remain unchanged.

Focused tests also cover packet queue ownership/backpressure and the actual
app publication policy: 760 packets/2,316,365 queue checks; 4096 status
combinations/12,308 wait checks; 655,360 adaptive-feed cases/1,966,080 checks,
including pointer wrap, the 127/128-word boundary, sync and DMA fallback. Those
checks alone imply neither a speed gain nor a physical timing pass.

Disposition: retain this as an unqualified source experiment. No production,
sibling game, SD card, normal profile or save was changed. No Quartus fit or
bitstream build was selected after the failed practical performance result.
Any future deployment first needs a reproducible net full-frame benefit,
strict native equivalence, physical area/timing/constraint qualification and
an isolated trial package preserving normal profiles and saves. No further
sweep or broader optimization is included in this bundle.
