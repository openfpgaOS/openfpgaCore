Standalone CPU area and timing probes, 2026-10-04
================================================

Scope
-----
These use a standalone VexiiRiscv top, virtual unconstrained external
interfaces, Cyclone V 5CEBA4F23C8, a 100 MHz constraint, saved seed 2, and
two Quartus processors. Settings match the earlier tiny-imf-v1 CPU probe.
They measure CPU area and internal register-to-register timing only.
They are neither complete os30 fits nor board/interface timing qualification.

The single-D64 control is the existing os30_worker CPU (single issue with a

generator comments and declaration whitespace, not RTL structure.
Both dual candidates keep the same caches, shared FPU/multiply/divide/LSU,
predictors and two-slot/sixteen-operation store buffer.

Lean adds 1272 ALMs to the standalone single control. Restricting its second
lane to immediate ALU operations saves 198 of those ALMs and two M10Ks.
These deltas must not be added to a whole-core report as guaranteed placement
savings; full os30 placement and timing are separate experiment results.

Diagnosis
---------
Lean's worst path is AlignerPlugin_logic_buffer_mask[0] to
decode_ctrls_1_up_Decode_INSTRUCTION_1[16]: second-instruction extraction and
compressed decoding, nine logic levels, 11.035 ns data delay.

Immediate's worst path is decode_ctrls_2_up_RS3_PHYS_0[4] through dependency
checking and dispatch backpressure to AlignerPlugin_logic_buffer_last[3],
10.820 ns data delay. Its second-instruction extraction/decompression path
still has -1.017 ns slack. Fixing decompression alone would leave the ready
path as another limit.

The grouped frontend rewrite adds 288 ALMs over lean and regresses internal
Fmax to 86.37 MHz. Its worst path remains buffer-mask to instruction1[15],
nine logic levels and 11.357 ns data delay. It is rejected as an area/timing
rescue. The smaller control-only rewrite adds 34 ALMs and improves setup by
only 0.113 ns: fetch_ctrl3.valid to instruction1[16] remains 10.977 ns, and
the RS1 dependency-to-aligner-ready path remains at -1.074 ns. Neither
rewrite establishes 100 MHz internal timing, let alone full-core closure.

The integer register file grows from two to eight M10Ks in lean, with about
85 ALMs directly attributed to its two-write-port storage selection. The
floating-point register file remains three M10Ks. The shared divider remains
about 190 ALMs, and the CPU retains four DSPs. Most additional ALMs are in
the flat main VexiiRiscv entity (decode, dispatch, operand selection, bypass
and pipeline control), not duplicated floating-point arithmetic. This is
hierarchy evidence, not precise attribution among those flattened functions.

Reproduction and evidence
-------------------------
  python3 tools/experiments/os30_dual_20261004/fit_cpu.py NEW_LABEL --cpu CPU.v

The utility refuses existing output directories and holds a campaign-wide
lock to prevent simultaneous standalone CPU fits. It does not run assembly,
change production RTL/configuration, deploy files or sweep seeds.

Artifacts live under docs/measurements/os30-dual-20261004/cpu-fit-LABEL/:
  manifest.json            exact input hashes, source identity and scope
  inputs/                  immutable initial RTL/project/timing inputs
  fit_cpu.py               exact executed utility source
  result.json              resources and all twenty timing-corner checks
  entity-resources.txt     complete hierarchy resource table
  fit/timing/              full setup/hold paths at four timing corners
  fit/output_files/        Quartus map/fit/STA reports

All five map/fit/STA stages passed. The four dual candidates fail internal
100 MHz setup checks; hold checks pass. The utility records that distinction
explicitly rather than treating a successful fitter exit as timing closure.

RVC stage1 moves expansion after raw instruction capture without adding a cycle.
RVC pipeline additionally separates ISA decode, adding a frontend pipeline stage.
Control variants factor exact-equivalent aligner/dispatch expressions.
Raw-FP classification saves no state and produced little further timing benefit.

The first skid variant broke the ready chain but failed flush/IRQ tests. Its
94.51 MHz result must not be presented as a correct or qualified CPU. The
corrected masked-skid variant gates stored lane masks on flush and passes the
full fourteen-case CPU suite. Its authoritative timing is the full-core fit,
not the rejected unmasked standalone probe above.
