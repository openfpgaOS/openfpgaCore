SM64 mesh area audit and exact refactors, 2026-10-04
==================================================

The v7 private prepared-raster core already disables the unused parametric
span/triangle commands, palette, original vertex transform/cache/derivation,
Q29 precision, command DMA and optional DOT/extended mesh operations. The
remaining depth, perspective, truecolor alpha blend, texel*C+D combiner,
texture cache, render cache and stream pipe are used by SM64. Disabling them
would change rendering or discard established throughput improvements.

One demonstrably unused physical feature remains: dedicated texture memory.
Current SM64 gfx_gpu.c allocates its textures using posix_memalign, writes its
SDRAM buffers and sends their physical addresses. It only prints FAST_TEX;
no texture-route/upload API is used. of_gpu_init() does not enable that route.
The private fit.py now has --no-texmem, removing INCLUDE_TEX_MEM consistently
from the frozen top/peripheral/GPU feature macros. Production defaults are
unchanged. v7 measured hierarchy costs are 170.7 ALMs for cram1_controller and
20.5 for gpu_cram1_tex_adapter, plus unmeasured BCR and GPU upload/route glue.
This is the dedicated GPU texture chip, not CPU RAM. Other applications must
obey capability bit25 and use their SDRAM fallback; the current trial remains
SM64-specific and is not a claim about their throughput.

No further broad GPU feature deletion is justified from the examined SM64
path. Eight v3 complete frames contain only commands 0x62, 0x63, 0x4A, 0x02,
0x11 and 0x42 before mesh expansion. This confirms these frames, not every
menu/level or another application's capability use.

Two exact arithmetic refactors, same ports and cycles:

1. gpu_mesh_vertex.v selects operands before one 64-bit adder. Matrix and
   light accumulation, unsigned light/fog correction, high-word accumulation
   and fog offset happen in mutually exclusive states. Every result remains
   the same modulo64 integer value, including extreme/fuzz lighting values.
   It does not assume a bounded real-game light vector or drop high bits.

2. gpu_mesh_project.v replaces a 64-bit product negation followed by addition
   with one 53-bit add/sub chain. The greatest-magnitude term is any signed64
   product shifted right12, which fits signed52. Adding a signed32 viewport
   center shifted left4 fits signed53. The Y terms (product>>16 and center)
   have a smaller bound. Saturation to signed16 occurs only after retaining
   the complete signed53 sum. This needs no captured viewport assumption.

Validation:
  vertex-unit-v3-shared-add: 65,264 captured/fuzz vertices, all 456,848 vertex
  words exact, 514 cancellation phases, unchanged 4,395,820 unit cycles.
  vertex-engine-v3-shared-add: 65,264 complete instruction programs, 3,002,144
  exact words and exact cycle counts, persistent scratch guards, following
  MUL/DIV, 90 cancellation phases and illegal-op behavior.
  project-engine-v3-area: 85,744 PROJ/PROJA programs, 2,143,600 exact words and
  exact cycles, arbitrary viewport/32-bit denominator boundaries, preserved
  context, shared-resource return and 150 cancellation phases.
  root whole-mesh-area-final-v1: 13,457 complete programs, 617,605 exact words
  and cycle counts, including the root's lean unused-op gates.
All evidence directories are under docs/measurements/os30-mesh-20261004/.

Root separately gated unused SEXT and plain PROJ47 in EXTENDED_ISA=0; PROJA51
remains. The full/default ISA is unchanged. No settings or arithmetic area
saving is claimed until the combined mesh-v8-area-notex fit reports it.
The nominal 191-ALM physical block cut and unmeasured arithmetic sharing do
not yet prove the approximately 720-ALM conservative placement target. No
memory-bit saving is counted as an ALM saving.
